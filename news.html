Put your tin-foil hat on... New conspiracy game will make you question reality!

"Are you sure you want to go through with this?" The first thing you are asked over an eerie communication system. What are we about to get into? The person explains, "Going into their databases could be a huge mistake." You are offered a link to follow, if you dare.

Clicking the link brings you to a solid black screen with a single word, OSWALD. Below the name is a password input field. Your communicator sends you another message. "This channel has been compromised. Change the frequency and I will give you the access code to get into the database."

OSWALD is a game based on intrigue, conspiracy, and the erosion of trust in your own beliefs and of those around you. The further you go into the game, the more you realize that things are NOT as they appear. You discover details about The Black Moth Conspiracy, a set of truths about a group of secret operators who are involved in various cover-ups and the disinformation programs of historical events. Your team also begins to unravel slowly as you realize that perhaps one of them may be secretly part of the conspiracy group. One by one, you will have to gather the clues and decide who on your team you can trust.

OSWALD is currently in development and new updates will be added once we have more news of the game.
